How it works

How it works, in four steps

1 Click save on anything. The extension button works on the page you are on. Details

Articles, videos, PDFs, posts, highlights

Save an article, a YouTube video, a podcast page, a PDF, a social post, or just the text you highlighted. For videos, captions are pulled from the page, so the brain has the words, not only the link. You can pick a folder like "AI tooling" or leave it in the main brain.

2 Or paste it. No extension needed on your phone or a work laptop. Details

A link or raw text, on the site

Open your brain on the website and paste a link, or paste any text: meeting notes, a newsletter, a thread someone sent you. It is saved the same way as a click from the extension.

3 It waits in your brain. Nothing pings you after each save. Details

Saves pile up quietly

Items are not processed one by one. They collect until your next issue is due, so the digest can see everything together and connect what belongs together.

4 Your issue arrives once or twice a week. You choose the day and time. Details

Five takeaways and a two-voice podcast

An agent groups related saves into five takeaways. Each one unfolds into about a page of detail, with "What to do with this" and links back to what you saved. It also writes a conversation for two voices: an interviewer asks, an analyst answers. You get it by email, in the app, or both.

Read it

Five TL;DR lines you can scan in a minute. Open the ones that matter for about a page of detail, a chart where it helps, and the saved sources it came from.

Listen to it

About ten minutes of two hosts: an interviewer who asks the questions you would ask, and an analyst who has read everything you saved. Good for a walk or the commute.

Why click, not automatic

The extension does not watch your browsing. Only what you choose to save leaves your browser, one click at a time. Everything else stays where it is.
