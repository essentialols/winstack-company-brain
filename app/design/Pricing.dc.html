Questions

Before you sign up

01 What counts as a save?

One click in the extension, or one paste on the site. A save can be a page, a video, a PDF, a post or a bit of highlighted text.

02 Does it read my browsing?

No. It only sees what you click save on. Nothing is collected in the background.

03 What is a brain?

A folder of saves with its own schedule. Keep one for "AI tooling" on Mondays and another for "Design" on Thursdays, for example.

04 Can I just listen?

Yes. On Pro, set your format to podcast only and each issue arrives as the two-voice episode.
